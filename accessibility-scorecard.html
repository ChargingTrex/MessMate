Tooling gap, stated up front

The wcag-audit skill synced without its scripts/ and references/ directories, so a11y_math.py, audit_page.py and build_dashboard.py were unavailable. There is also no axe-core, Playwright or Node on this machine. The WCAG arithmetic was reimplemented from the normative formulas and self-tested against published reference values before use — including the canonical boundary greys (#767676 on white = 4.54:1, #595959 on white = 7.00:1, black on white = 21.00:1). Every number here is reproducible from those formulas.

Three measurement errors found and corrected

Recorded because each would have produced a false finding:

  1. Headless Chrome refuses to size its window below ~500 CSS px, so the first reflow pass silently measured 500px and reported a false pass. Redone with Emulation.setDeviceMetricsOverride.
  2. Flagging animation-name != none under reduced motion produced 4 false failures on /home. The guard zeroes duration and iteration count, so the name survives while the motion does not. Re-measured as duration × iterations.
  3. A focus probe reading computed styles immediately after .focus() returned pre-transition values (transition: border-color 0.2s), making every border look unchanged. Re-measured after the transition settles.

Two apparent failures investigated and dismissed

  1. /home's three suggestion inputs are unreachable by Tab because they sit inside a closed <details> disclosure. The <summary> is reachable and the input becomes focusable when opened — a correct pattern, not a defect.
  2. button#submit-btn measures 1.91:1, but it is disabled on load and SC 1.4.3 exempts inactive components. Retained as advisory only.

Not tested

Screen-reader output (NVDA / JAWS / VoiceOver), so 4.1.2 is assessed from accessible names in the DOM rather than from what a screen reader announces. 1.2.x is not applicable — no audio or video.
