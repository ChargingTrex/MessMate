How to use MessMate

A step-by-step picture guide for management, deans and the Food Committee. Every screen, and every button you need to click.

Before you start

MessMate collects feedback on the mess food in three separate ways and shows it to the people who can act on it. The three are never averaged together.

How to read this guide

1A red number on a screenshot matches the same red number in the text beside or above it.
A red outline shows the button or box you click or type in.
BoldBold text is the part you must not miss, or the exact name of a button.

The screenshots use sample data. The names, scores and menus are examples. Your screens will show your own mess.

The three kinds of feedback

Quick taps
Students tap Good, Bad or Skipped once per meal. No sign-in. Shown as the % who liked the meal.
Dish form
Students score lunch and each dish from 1 to 5. No sign-in. Shown as an average out of 5.
Committee review
Food Committee members, signed in and named, score every dish and five areas for each meal. Shown as an average out of 5.

Sign in and set your password

Everyone signs in on the same page. Your email decides which pages you get; you do not choose a role. Students never sign in.

Address: your MessMate link followed by /signinUsed by: admin, dean, committee members

Sign in

Use the account you were given.

  1. Type your email.
  2. Type your password. A new committee member types the one-time password the admin sent.
  3. Click Sign in.

Where you land. Admin opens the Overview. A dean opens the Student feedback dashboard. A committee member opens the committee dashboard.

First time only: choose your own password

Committee members see this page the first time they sign in, and again after the admin resets their password.

  1. Type a new password of at least 8 characters.
  2. Type the same password again.
  3. Click Save and continue.

You cannot review a meal until you do this. If you forget your password later, ask the admin to reset it.

The top bar

The bar at the top of every page is how you move around. It shows different links depending on who is signed in.

Signed in as admin

  1. Overview: today on one screen.
  2. Menu: publish the weekly menu.
  3. Members: add and manage committee members.
  4. Students: the student feedback dashboard.
  5. Committee: the committee dashboard.
  6. Sign out: always sign out on a shared computer.
  7. Light / Dark: switches the screen colours. This guide shows the dark screen; the light one works the same way.

Signed in as a dean or viewer

  1. Students: the student feedback dashboard.
  2. Committee: the committee dashboard.
  3. Sign out.

Were you sent a dashboard link instead? A link that contains ?token= opens the same two dashboards without signing in. Do not forward it: anyone with the link can read the dashboards.

Signed in as a committee member

  1. Menu: today's menu, the page students see.
  2. Rate dish: the students' dish form.
  3. Dashboard: your committee dashboard.
  4. Committee: review a meal.

Overview: today at the mess

The first page an admin sees. It puts today on one screen and tells you what needs a decision.

Top bar: OverviewAddress: /adminUsed by: admin

Start with Needs attention

  1. Needs attention lists everything that needs a decision today. If the list is empty, nothing is waiting.
  2. Click the link at the end of a line to go straight to the page that fixes it.
  3. Today's numbers start here and run across the page. Each card is one kind of feedback. A grey -- means no data yet, not zero.
  4. Edit this week's menu opens the menu editor.

What puts a line in Needs attention

  • A meal today or in the next six days is not published.
  • Students score lunch or a dish below 2.5 out of 5.
  • Fewer than 40% liked a meal, once five students have rated it.
  • The committee scored an area below 2.5.
  • Members have not reviewed today. Shown from 3 pm.
  • A member has never signed in to set a password.

Today's menu and the week ahead

  1. Edit opens the menu editor for this week. The coloured bar under each meal shows students' quick taps.
  2. Publication shows the next seven days. A green dot is published. A dotted red ring is not published. B, L and D are breakfast, lunch and dinner.
  3. Open editor takes you to the menu editor to fill the gaps.

Who has reviewed, and the latest feedback

  1. Dashboard opens the full committee dashboard. The list shows each member as Reviewed or Not yet.
  2. All suggestions opens the student dashboard. Each comment is labelled with where it came from.

Publish the weekly menu

Students only see a menu once it is published. A meal left blank shows them “Menu not published yet”.

Top bar: MenuAddress: /admin/menuUsed by: admin

Pick the week and fill it quickly

The page shows one card per day, Monday to Sunday. The line under the title counts how many meals are published.

  1. Prev, This week and Next move between weeks.
  2. Fill blanks from template copies the standard weekly menu into every empty box.
  3. Copy last week copies last week's published menu into every empty box.
  4. Save all 7 days publishes the whole week in one click.

Nothing you typed is overwritten. Both fill buttons only touch empty boxes. Nothing is published until you click a Save button.

Publish one day

  1. The badge shows the day's state: Published, 2 of 3 published or Not published.
  2. Type the dishes, separated by commas, for example Idli, Sambar, Chutney. Faint slanted text is only a hint: that meal is not published.
  3. Use template fills this day's empty boxes with the standard menu.
  4. Click Save to publish this day.

Students see it straight away. A saved menu appears on the student page immediately.

Or import the week from a spreadsheet file

  1. Click Download blank template. You get a CSV file for the week on screen, with the columns Date, Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.
  2. Fill it in Excel or Google Sheets, save it as a CSV, then click Choose file and pick it.
  3. Click Upload CSV.

Rules for the file. Dates are written YYYY-MM-DD. Keep each meal's dishes in one cell, separated by commas. The file must be under 1 MB. A date that already has a menu is updated, not duplicated. A blank cell publishes nothing. Rows with a bad date are skipped and listed.

Three ways to publish, one result

WayBest forStudents see it
Menu editorDay-to-day changes, or filling a week from the templateImmediately after Save
CSV importA whole week planned in a spreadsheetImmediately after Upload CSV
The Google SheetStaff who prefer to type into the menu tab, one row per dateWithin 30 seconds

MessMate never shows students a made-up menu. The standard weekly menu is only a typing aid for you. Until you save, students see “Menu not published yet”.

Committee members

Add the people who review the food, hand them a one-time password, and retire them when they leave.

Top bar: MembersAddress: /admin/membersUsed by: admin

Add one member

  1. Type the member's name.
  2. Type their email. This is what they sign in with.
  3. Click Add member.

Copy the one-time password straight away

  1. The one-time password appears at the top of the page.
  2. Copy all copies every new password at once. Send each member theirs privately.

It is shown only once. If you leave the page without copying it, it cannot be shown again. Use Reset password to make a new one.

Read and manage the list

  1. Password not set means the member has not signed in yet.
  2. Reset password gives a locked-out member a new one-time password.
  3. Deactivate a member who leaves the committee. Reactivate brings them back.
  4. A tick means the member has reviewed today. A dot means not yet.

There is no delete. Deactivating keeps a member's past reviews. To change the committee, deactivate the outgoing members and add the new ones.

Add many members at once

Use this at the start of a new committee.

  1. Type or paste one member per line, as Name, email.
  2. Click Add all.

A bad line does not stop the rest. Lines with a problem are listed separately and the others are still added.

Review a meal

Five steps, done once for each meal every day. Reviews are not anonymous: your name is saved with every review.

Top bar: CommitteeAddress: /committeeUsed by: committee members

Pick a meal

  1. A green meal marked ✓ Reviewed is done.
  2. An amber meal marked To review still needs you. Click it.

The page chooses for you. It opens on the latest meal served that you have not reviewed.

Rate every dish

Each dish on today's published menu has its own card. Click one face per dish, from 1 (worst, left) to 5 (best, right).

  1. Clicking a face selects it. The selected face is highlighted.
  2. Do the same for every dish in the list.

No dishes listed? The menu for that meal is not published. Rate only the five areas in the next step.

Rate the five areas

  1. Click one face for each area, the same way as the dishes.
TasteFlavour and seasoning
QualityFreshness, temperature and cooking
VarietyRange and balance across the counters
HygieneCleanliness of food, counters and utensils
MenuDid the meal match the published menu?

Add a note and submit

  1. Write a short review if it would help the kitchen act. It is optional, up to 500 characters.
  2. Click Submit review.

Is the button faded and not clickable? You have missed a dish or an area. The line under the button tells you how many are rated.

Review the next meal

  1. Click a meal name to review a meal you still have to do today.
  2. Committee dashboard shows how the committee scored today.

Asked to rate again? If the mess office changes that meal's menu while you are rating, your review is not saved. The page shows the new dishes so no score is filed under the wrong dish.

Committee member dashboard

What a committee member sees after signing in: the committee's scores, and their own record.

Top bar: DashboardAddress: /committee/dashboardUsed by: committee members

Today's scores and your own count

The first four cards are the whole committee: today's score, how many members have reviewed, the 7-day average and the lowest-scoring area.

  1. My reviews this month counts the meal reviews you have filed.
  2. My last review shows your most recent score and its date.
  3. Sign out is here, at the top right of the page.

Your record, the committee, and what students said

  1. My record repeats your last review, area by area, with what you wrote.
  2. Reviewed today lists every member. Your own name is marked (you).

Below these two cards, What students said today is shown for context only and is never mixed into the committee score. Beside it, Committee Reviews lists the written reviews, newest first, with the member's name, date and meal.

Student feedback dashboard

Everything students told you, from the dish form and from the quick taps. The two are shown in separate parts of the page.

Top bar: StudentsAddress: /dashboardUsed by: admin, dean

The dish form numbers

  1. Today's average is students' overall score out of 5. The small line under it compares with yesterday.
  2. Best dish today, with its score.
  3. Lowest dish today. This is the dish to look into first.

The trend and each dish's score

  1. Click 1 month to see 30 days instead of 7. Click 7 days to go back.

Quick taps, meal by meal

  1. The bar shows the taps for that meal: green is Good, red is Bad. The figures on the right are Good, Bad and Skipped, then the % liked.

Skips do not lower the score. The percentage counts only students who ate the meal.

What students asked for

The left list holds suggestions typed on the dish form. The right list holds notes added to a quick tap, each tagged with its meal. Both are anonymous.

Committee dashboard

How the Food Committee scored the mess, who took part, and what they wrote.

Top bar: CommitteeAddress: /dashboard/committeeUsed by: admin, dean

Score and participation

  1. Participation is the share of active members who filed a review today.
  2. Lowest today names the weakest of the five areas, and the best one beside it.
  3. Manage members opens the Members page. Only an admin sees this button.

The five areas and the trend

  1. Click 1 month to see 30 days instead of 7.

The left chart shows today's score for each of the five areas. A bar that is amber or red is the area to raise with the kitchen. Click an area name under the right chart to add its line to the trend.

Who reviewed, and what they wrote

Reviewed today lists every active member. Committee Reviews shows each written review with the member's name, the date and the meal, so you can follow up with the right person.

What students see

Students open this page on a phone and never sign in.

Address: /homeUsed by: everyone, no sign-in

Today's menu and one tap per meal

  1. The day tabs show the rest of the week's menu. Only today can be rated.
  2. Each meal has three buttons: Good, Bad and Skipped. A student taps one after eating.
  3. Sign in is where you, the committee and management sign in.
RuleWhat it means

Only after the meal startsButtons stay grey until serving time: breakfast 7:30 am, lunch 12:00 pm, dinner 7:30 pm
Undo for 10 minutesA student can press Undo within 10 minutes. After that the tap is final
“Menu not published yet”The mess office has not published that meal. Students can still rate it after eating
