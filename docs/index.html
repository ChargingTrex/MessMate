MessMate User Guide

MessMate collects feedback on college mess food in three separate streams and surfaces clear, actionable data to the people who can act on it.

Quick Taps

1 tap · 3 meals / day

Dish Review

1-5 scale · every 4 hours

Committee Audit

5 areas · per-meal audit

Data Isolation

Never averaged together

🚀 Getting Started & Roles #

Depending on your role in the campus community, you will access different parts of MessMate. Find your role below to jump to the right instructions:

You are Start here Sign-in needed Role Purpose
🎓 A student /home (Today's menu) No sign-in Check menus, tap 1-click sentiment, rate lunch dishes
👥 A food committee member /signin Yes (Assigned account) Conduct per-meal quality audits across 5 areas
🛠️ An administrator /signin or /admin/login Yes (Admin password) Manage menus, roster, and inspect operational metrics
👔 A dean or read-only viewer /signin or token link Yes / Secure token Inspect high-level trends and committee ratings

⚖️ The Three Feedback Streams #

Key Architectural Principle: The three kinds of feedback are kept strictly separate on purpose and are never averaged together. A tally of quick taps, a mean of 1 to 5 dish scores, and an authenticated committee review measure different things from different people.
⚡
1. Student Quick Taps
Immediate volume sentiment (Good / Bad / Skipped). Answers: "Did students enjoy eating this meal today?"
🍲
2. Dish-by-Dish Reviews
Granular lunch dish ratings (1-5 scale) + text suggestions. Answers: "Which specific preparation worked or failed?"
📋
3. Committee Audits
Structured inspection of taste, quality, variety, hygiene, and menu compliance with attributable member accountability.

🎓 For Students #

🔒 Privacy First: Zero Tracking

Nothing you submit is linked to you. MessMate stores no name, no email address, no roll number, and no IP address with your feedback.

You can express your honest feedback without any concern of tracking or repercussions.

1. Checking the Daily Menu

Open /home on your phone or computer. It shows today's breakfast, lunch, and dinner along with serving times:

Meal Standard Serving Window Rating Eligibility
Breakfast 7:30 am to 9:30 am Opens at 7:30 am, remains open for the day
Lunch 12:00 pm to 2:30 pm Opens at 12:00 pm, remains open for the day
Dinner 7:30 pm to 9:30 pm Opens at 7:30 pm, remains open for the day
  • Day Tabs: Switch tabs at the top to preview menus for other days of the week.
  • Unpublished Menus: If a card displays "Menu not published yet", the mess kitchen has not posted the items yet. The meal is still being served, and you can still submit your rating after eating!
  • Ticker Banner: The moving banner under the header includes a Pause button on its right side.

2. Rate a Meal in One Tap

Each meal card provides three instant rating buttons:

  • 👍 Good: You ate it and liked it.
  • 👎 Bad: You ate it and didn't like it.
  • ⏭️ Skipped: You didn't eat this meal in the mess.
Adding a Suggestion: Want to explain why? Open Add a suggestion and type your thoughts before tapping Good, Bad, or Skipped. Your suggestion is transmitted together with your tap.

3. Rating Rules & 10-Minute Undo Window

1

One tap per meal per day

Each phone or browser can rate Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner once each (up to 3 quick taps daily).

2

Serving hours gate

Buttons stay disabled until that meal's serving time starts. Once open, it remains open for the rest of the calendar day.

3

Today only

Other days' menus can be explored, but can never be rated.

4

10-minute Undo Guarantee

Tapped by mistake? Click Undo on the meal card within 10 minutes. Your rating is wiped and the buttons reappear. After 10 minutes, ratings lock permanently.

4. Rate Lunch Dish by Dish

For detailed feedback on lunch preparations, tap Rate dish by dish at the bottom of the home page, or go directly to /.

  • Overall Score (Required): Select one of five emoji faces from 😡 (1) to 😄 (5).
  • Individual Dishes (Optional): Rice with curry, rice with rasam, chapati, chapati with gravy, poriyal, sweet/fruit, salad, curd, papad, pickle/thogayal. Rate only what you tasted.
  • Comments (Optional): Up to 150 characters of constructive critique.
  • Suggest a Dish (Optional): Request specific dishes you want the mess to prepare.
  • Frequency Limit: One submission every 4 hours per device.

5. Messages You Might See

Message Prompt Reason & Resolution
You've already rated lunch today This device already submitted a rating. Use Undo within 10 minutes if you want to alter it.
Dinner isn't being served yet The meal's serving window has not started. Please revisit after eating.
Ratings open on the day itself You are browsing another day's menu. Switch back to the Today tab.
This device has used today's three quick ratings All three meals have been rated from this device. Come back tomorrow or rate lunch dish by dish.
That rating is more than 10 minutes old The 10-minute grace period for Undo has expired. The score is permanent.
You've already submitted a review in the last 4 hours The dish-by-dish form enforces a 4-hour cooldown between reviews on the same device.
Couldn't save that just now Temporary network disruption. Tap the button once again.

👥 For Food Committee Members #

Attributable Reviews: Unlike student feedback, committee reviews are not anonymous. Your name and verified email are saved alongside every review so the mess administration and management can follow up with you.

1. First-Time Sign In

  1. The admin adds your account and shares a secure one-time password.
  2. Open /signin and enter your registered email and temporary password.
  3. You will immediately be prompted to create your own permanent password (minimum 8 characters). You cannot submit reviews until this step is completed.
  4. Sign out anytime from the top bar button. If you forget your password, contact the administrator for a one-click reset.

2. Auditing a Meal

Navigate to /committee. You inspect each meal separately, once per meal per day.

1

Select a Meal

The tabs at the top show Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner. A meal you still have to review is yellow, To review; one you've reviewed turns green, ✓ Reviewed. The page opens on the meal most recently served that you haven't reviewed yet.

2

Rate Dishes on the Published Menu

Every dish on today's published menu for that meal gets its own card. Score each one from 1 to 5. The dishes change with the meal and the day. If the menu has not been published yet, there are no dish cards and you rate only the five areas.

3

Score the 5 Quality Pillars

Rate Taste, Quality, Variety, Hygiene and Menu from 1 to 5.

4

Add a Written Review (Optional)

Up to 500 characters on what worked, what didn't, and what the kitchen should change.

5

Submit

Submit enables only once every dish and all five areas are rated. The thank-you page links to any meal you still have to review today.

If the menu changes while you rate: if someone edits that meal's menu while you are filling in your review, MessMate does not save it. It shows the updated dishes and asks you to rate them again, so scores are never filed under the wrong dish.

3. The 5 Core Quality Pillars

👅
1. Taste
Flavour and seasoning of what was served.
✨
2. Quality
Freshness, temperature, and cooking.
🥗
3. Variety
Range and balance across the counters.
🧼
4. Hygiene
Cleanliness of food, counters, and utensils.
📜
5. Menu
Did the meal match the published menu?

4. Committee Member Dashboard

Visit /committee/dashboard to review your team's ongoing audits:

  • Today's Committee Score: Average score, how many members have reviewed (and how many meal reviews that adds up to), the 7-day average, and the lowest-scoring area.
  • Your Own Record: Your meal reviews this month and your last review.
  • Student Voice Context: What students reported today is surfaced in a dedicated side card for reference without blending into your audit.
  • Recent Committee Reviews: Written reviews from members, newest first, each labelled with its date and meal.

🛠️ For Administrators #

1. Administrative Command Center

Sign in at /signin using the administrative credentials or directly at /admin/login. The main dashboard at /admin aggregates operations in a single view:

  • Today's lunch dish-by-dish score and quick-tap sentiment per meal.
  • Committee audit score, active participation count, and 7-day trends.
  • Menu publishing status across the upcoming 7 calendar days.

2. Automated "Needs Attention" Flags

The top of /admin displays urgent alerts that require immediate administrative intervention:

Alert Condition Trigger Logic Direct Remediation Link
Today's Menu Missing Any meal today is blank or unpublished /admin/menu
Upcoming Menu Incomplete Unpublished meal found in next 6 days /admin/menu
Low Student Rating Today's lunch or a specific dish falls below 2.5 / 5.0 /dashboard
Low Meal Sentiment Fewer than 40% of students liked a meal (min 5 ratings) /dashboard
Committee Quality Alarm Committee rates any quality pillar below 2.5 / 5.0 /dashboard/committee
Missing Committee Audits From 3 pm, active members who haven't reviewed any meal today /dashboard/committee
Pending Onboarding Active members who haven't signed in to set their own password /admin/members

3. Publishing Menus (3 Seamless Ways)

Students only see dishes once published; blank meals explicitly show "Menu not published yet". All three methods synchronize directly to the same backend:

Method 1: The Visual Weekly Editor (/admin/menu)

Cards for each day Monday to Sunday:

  • Navigate weeks with ◀ Prev, This week, and Next ▶.
  • Type dish names separated by commas (e.g. Idli, Sambar, Coconut Chutney) or line breaks.
  • Fill blanks from template: Copies the standard rotational baseline into empty cells without overwriting typed text.
  • Copy last week: Copies the previous week's live menu into blank slots.
  • Click Save on an individual day or Save all 7 days at once.
Method 2: Bulk CSV Import
  1. Click Download blank template for the selected week.
  2. Columns: Date, Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner with dates in YYYY-MM-DD format. Separate dishes in cells with commas.
  3. Save as a UTF-8 CSV (< 1MB) and click Upload CSV. Menus are parsed and updated instantly.
Method 3: Direct Google Sheet Sync

Mess staff can type directly into the menu tab of the connected Google Sheet: one row per date with Date in Column A. Edits reflect in MessMate within 30 seconds.

4. Committee Member Roster (/admin/members)

  • Add Member: Enter name and email to generate an instant one-time login.
  • Bulk Import: Paste lists of members as Name, email. MessMate validates rows, adds valid members, and flags any syntax errors.
  • Copy One-Time Passwords: Copy passwords immediately upon creation. Only cryptographic hashes are stored, so they cannot be retrieved later.
  • Password Reset: Issue a replacement one-time password if a member gets locked out.
  • Deactivate / Reactivate: Never delete members; deactivation preserves historical audit trails and committee records across academic rotations.

5. Executive Dashboards

Dashboard URL Primary Metrics
Student Feedback /dashboard Lunch dish scores, 7-day trendlines, total sample volume, top-rated vs lowest-rated dishes, quick-tap percentages, and student suggestion feed.
Committee Audits /dashboard/committee Committee score, members reviewed and participation, 7-day average, lowest area, today by area, 7-day trend, who has reviewed today, and the written reviews.

6. Color Bands & Interpretation Standard

Scores and percentages are coloured the same way on every dashboard:

Red: Score < 2.5 or Tap < 40%
Amber: Score 2.5 – 3.5 or Tap 40% – 69%
Green: Score > 3.5 or Tap ≥ 70%
Grey ("--"): No responses yet (not a zero)

👔 For Deans & Read-Only Viewers #

University leadership, wardens, and mess supervisors who require operational oversight have two secure ways to access executive reports:

Option A: Read-Only Account Login

Sign in at /signin using your viewer credentials. You have read access to /dashboard and /dashboard/committee without permissions to alter menus or edit members.

Option B: Secure Token Link

You may be provided an executive URL containing a secure access token (/dashboard?token=…). This allows immediate, credential-free dashboard inspection on mobile or tablet.

Security Warning: Do not forward your token link publicly. Anyone with the token parameter can view dashboard data.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions #

Can I change my quick-tap rating after submitting?

Yes, but only within 10 minutes. Click the Undo button on the meal card on /home. After 10 minutes, the score is locked in permanently to ensure dataset integrity.

Why are student quick taps and committee audits separate?

Student quick taps measure high-volume emotional sentiment from hundreds of diners, while committee reviews rate each dish and five quality areas, meal by meal, with a name attached. Averaging them together would dilute both signals.

What happens if a meal menu isn't uploaded?

MessMate never shows made-up food. The card displays "Menu not published yet". Students can still rate the meal after eating, and committee members rate the 5 quality areas without individual dish breakdowns.
