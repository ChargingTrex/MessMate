IlaiMessMate's design system

Ilai is Tamil and Malayalam for leaf. The system takes its look from a South Indian meal served on a banana leaf: the leaf is the brand, rice is the ground, and every side dish brings its own colour, from turmeric sambar and chilli rasam to beetroot poriyal and carrot pickle.

Colour, served

Sixteen colours from the leaf, the rice and the side dishes. Each plate shows the text colour that sits on it and the contrast it reaches.

Shape and space

Corners follow the Material 3 shape scale and spacing follows a 4px grid. The leaf is soft-cornered; nothing on it is sharp.

Against Material 3

Ilai follows the Material 3 structure (roles, scales, states and targets) and keeps its own look where the meal needs it. Each departure below is deliberate.

Rules

How to lay the leaf so it stays easy to read.

Colour

  • Build with roles, not dish names. A card is surface-container-high, not leaf-pale.
  • primary is the one main action per view. Tonal and outlined buttons carry the rest.
  • Let colour own whole regions: section bands, plates and containers. Don't sprinkle it in small accents.
  • One side dish per component. A turmeric badge and a chilli alert never share a card.
  • Turmeric and carrot are fills with ink text. Never use them as text or lines on rice.
  • Chilli and leaf have nearly the same lightness, so every status also carries a word or an icon.

Type

  • Arima for display and headlines only. Hind Madurai for titles, body and labels.
  • One display-large per page. Body text stays between 45 and 75 characters per line.
  • Sentence case everywhere. No tracked capitals.

Shape and space

  • Buttons and segments are shape-full, chips shape-s, cards shape-l, sheets shape-xl.
  • Spacing comes only from the 4px grid.
  • Separate surfaces with a tonal step and a 1px edge, not a shadow.

Focus and states

  • The focus ring is a solid 2px focus outline with a 2px offset: ink by day, curd-white by night.
  • Hover, focus and press are layers of the content colour: 8%, 10% and 10%.
  • Disabled controls drop to 12% fill and 50% text, and lose their state layer.

Voice

Plain and warm, like the person serving: "Lunch is ready", "Rate this meal", "Thanks, noted". Second person and sentence case.

Logo and icons

The logo is the hero's banana leaf, reduced: the leaf, its midrib and veins, a mound of rice with sambar, and an appalam. It sits left of the wordmark, on leaf-deep or rice, and never below 24px wide. Icons are drawn in a single 24px solid set, never emoji.

Tokens

Copy these into a stylesheet. The roles are the light scheme; the dark scheme overrides the same names under [data-scheme="dark"].

:root {
  /* Colour roles: light */
  --primary: #2f6b2a;               --on-primary: #f6f2e4;
  --primary-container: #cdeab6;     --on-primary-container: #1d4620;
  --secondary: #8e2450;             --on-secondary: #f6f2e4;
  --secondary-container: #f7dfe8;   --on-secondary-container: #6e1a3d;
  --tertiary: #e2a320;              --on-tertiary: #211a12;
  --tertiary-container: #fbedc4;    --on-tertiary-container: #5c3f00;
  --error: #b02a1c;                 --on-error: #f6f2e4;
  --error-container: #fadcd5;       --on-error-container: #7d1b10;
  --surface: #f6f2e4;               --on-surface: #211a12;
  --surface-container-lowest: #fffcf2;
  --surface-container-low: #f1efdd;
  --surface-container: #ebecd4;
  --surface-container-high: #e1e9c9;
  --surface-container-highest: #d6e0b8;
  --on-surface-variant: #5c4834;
  --outline: #5c4834;               --outline-variant: #cfc6a6;
  --inverse-surface: #1d4620;       --inverse-on-surface: #f6f2e4;
  --inverse-primary: #9ed58c;

  /* Shape */
  --shape-xs: 4px; --shape-s: 8px; --shape-m: 12px;
  --shape-l: 16px; --shape-xl: 28px; --shape-full: 999px;

  /* Space: 4px grid */
  --space-1: 4px; --space-2: 8px; --space-3: 12px; --space-4: 16px;
  --space-6: 24px; --space-8: 32px; --space-12: 48px; --space-16: 64px;

  /* Type */
  --font-display: Arima, Georgia, serif;
  --font-text: "Hind Madurai", system-ui, sans-serif;
}
